Understanding Alzheimer's Disease

Alzheimer's disease is a progressive neurological disorder that causes brain cells to degenerate and die, leading to a continuous decline in thinking, behavioral, and social skills.

Non Demented

Normal cognitive function. No detectable signs of dementia in brain scans.

Very Mild

Early memory lapses. Subtle cognitive changes that may go unnoticed.

Mild

Noticeable memory loss. Difficulty with daily tasks and planning.

Moderate

Significant decline. Requires full-time care and specialist treatment.
